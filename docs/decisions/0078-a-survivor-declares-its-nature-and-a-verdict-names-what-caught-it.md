# A survivor declares its nature, and a verdict names what caught it

## Context and Problem Statement

A survivor, and the kind of thing it is. A count of surviving cells published on its own is read as a
count of holes, and most of them are not holes.

## Considered Options

- Publish the survivor count, and let the descriptions explain each one.
- Require a nature on every survivor, as an argument rather than an optional field.

## Decision Outcome

**The nature is an argument rather than a field somebody wanted, and the argument is about how a
survivor reads from outside.** A count of surviving cells published on its own is read as a count of
holes, and that is not what these are: a mutant nothing could ever catch, a behaviour the contract
deliberately declines to specify, a rule no input in this catalogue exercises, and a limit this
repository has already declared with its price are four different things, and only the fourth is a
debt. Aggregating them publishes a number more frightening than the truth, which is as much a misreport
as one that flatters.

It is a required argument rather than an optional field for the reason `DeferredNeed.until` is required
one folder along: a rule in prose is one the next declaration omits, and a survivor whose nature nobody
stated is exactly the survivor that gets counted as a hole. Making it unwritable costs one word at each
of the sites that already argue the point in their own description.

**The description goes on carrying the argument and this carries only the kind.** Two statements of one
thing drift, and the description is where every one of these sites already says why — so this adds what
a count can be taken over and nothing else.

### The one survivor that declares no nature

The survivor the apparatus explains, which is the only one that declares no nature.

A lens removes part of the suite's sight; a defect that dies on the column reading the contract as
committed and lives on a blinded one is *the measurement the lens exists for* — what a contract without
that half of its surface would have caught, which is nothing. Declaring a nature here would be
inventing a fact about the contract out of a setting of the instrument.

It carries no nature rather than a fifth member saying "the apparatus", because that member would be
writable on a cell where it is false. What makes it legitimate is structural and is checked:
`survivorFaults` refuses a bare survivor whose own mutant does not die on the committed column.

### Killed by the compiler and by nothing else

Killed by the compiler and by nothing else: the run reddened and no guard reported a failure, because
the defect never reached one.

It is a real kill and it is counted apart, because what caught it is not a guard anyone wrote. A
specification battery produces these by construction — removing a reason literal breaks the union every
table entry is typed against — and the share of a contract that only this verdict holds is worth
reading rather than folding into the score. The type system is part of the verification; pretending a
test did the work would misattribute it.

### A probe is not a defect

A probe: a question about the shape of the contract rather than a defect of it, kept out of the score
so that a score measures the contract and not the question.

## Consequences

Four verdicts and four natures, none of which can be left unstated, so no published total is an
aggregate of things that are not alike. The cost is one word per site, and every site was already
arguing the point in prose.

## Confirmation

`a-survivor-with-no-nature-and-no-blinded-lens-is-refused` is the structural check that keeps the bare
form honest; `every-survivor-is-accounted-for-by-a-nature-or-by-a-lens` establishes the totality; and
`every-cell-published-as-surviving-is-pinned-as-surviving` establishes that the published set and the
pinned set are one.

## What would reopen this

A fifth kind of survivor. The four were found by writing batteries rather than by enumerating
possibilities, so a fifth is likelier to arrive the same way — and the union is what makes it a compile
error rather than a silent fifth case.
